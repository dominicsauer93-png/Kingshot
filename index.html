    Where to get Hero Gear materials
    • Enhancement XP: Bear Hunt; feeding spare gear (Grey 10, Green 30, Blue 60, Purple 150)
    • Forgehammers: Bear Hunt, event shops, Buccaneer Bounty, Kingdom of Power, Strongest Governor, Merchant Empire, Alliance Mobilization, Honor Fund, packs
    • Mithril: Arena Shop — 10,000 Arena Tokens each, max 3 per week
    • Mythic Gear: Mastery 11–20 and red-gear imbuements
    Mithril is needed at every red milestone (120, 140, 160, 180, 200) — stockpile it. Keep mastery one step behind your next imbuement.
    Where to get Widgets
    • Chests: Custom Hero Widget Chest (Buccaneer Bounty, Champagne Fair)
    • Shops: Mystery Shop
    • Other: Hall of Heroes, event rewards, packs
    Lv 0 → 10 costs 275 widgets (5, 10, 15 … 50 per level). Keep your three rally heroes level — lowest first.
    Where to get Charm Guides & Designs
    • Events: Eternity's Reach, Alliance Brawl, Merchant Empire, Kingdom of Power, Strongest Governor, Officer Project, Champagne Fair
    • Shops: Swordland, Tidal, Kingdom of Power, Alliance
    • Other: Crystal Cave, packs
    Designs are usually the bottleneck. Infantry charms first, then Archers.

    How pets level up
    • Levelling: Pet Food raises level. Every 10 levels the pet must Advance (Growth Manuals, later Nutrient Potions and Medallions)
    • Refining: Common or Advanced Taming Marks; secondary buffs go up or down at random
    • Where to get: daily play, Pet Adventures, some pet skills, events, packs
    Before leading a rally, turn on Panther, Elephant, Great Moose, Bison and War Bear skills. Save Advanced Taming Marks for high-level pets.
    Where to get Gold Hero Shards
    • Modes: Arena leaderboard, advanced Intel Missions, Conquest stages
    • Shops: VIP Shop, Swordland Shop, Hall of Heroes
    • Other: recruitment, fishing & events, packs
    General Mythic shards fit any Mythic hero — except Amadeus and Helga.
