        Where to get Hero Gear materials
        • Enhancement XP: Bear Hunt (the "Leader by Example" skill adds bonus parts per hunt); feeding spare gear also gives XP — Grey 10, Green 30, Blue 60, Purple 150
        • Forgehammers: Bear Hunt, event shops, Buccaneer Bounty, Kingdom of Power, Strongest Governor, Merchant Empire, Alliance Mobilization, Honor Fund, packs
        • Mithril: Arena Shop — 10,000 Arena Tokens each, max 3 per week
        • Mythic Gear: used for Mastery 11–20 and for red-gear ascension milestones

        Mithril is needed at every 20-level red-gear milestone (120, 140, 160, 180, 200) and is the usual long-term bottleneck — stockpile it early. Forgehammers can't be recovered once spent.

        Where to get Charm Guides & Designs
        • Events: Eternity's Reach, Alliance Brawl, Merchant Empire, Kingdom of Power, Strongest Governor, Officer Project, Champagne Fair
        • Shops: Swordland, Tidal, Kingdom of Power, Alliance
        • Other: Crystal Cave, packs & deals

        Designs are usually the bottleneck. Common advice: get every charm to Lv 3 first, then push your main troop's charms to Lv 5.

        Widgets in hand vs. needed to max every hero
        Where to get Widgets
        • Chests: Custom Hero Widget Chest from Buccaneer Bounty and Champagne Fair
        • Shops: Mystery Shop
        • Other: Hall of Heroes (later generations), limited-time event rewards, packs & deals

        Lv 0 → 10 costs 275 widgets (5, 10, 15 … 50 per level).

        All heroes
        Where to get Gold (Mythic) Hero Shards
        • Modes: Arena leaderboard, advanced Intel Missions, certain Conquest stages
        • Shops: VIP Shop, Swordland Shop
        • Other: hero recruitment, fishing & special events, packs & deals

        General Mythic shards can go to any Mythic hero — except Amadeus and Helga, who only take their own shards.

        Materials in hand
        How pets level up & where to get materials
        • Levelling: Pet Food raises level (Attack/Defense buffs). Every 10 levels the pet must Advance using Growth Manuals, and later Nutrient Potions
        • Refining: uses Common or Advanced Taming Marks; each refine randomly raises or lowers secondary buffs
        • Where to get: daily play and Pet Adventures (daily chests), some pets' active skills (Pet Food), events and packs

        Save Advanced Taming Marks for high-level Epic (purple) and Legendary (gold) refinement — they're wasted on early tiers.

        Setup steps
        1. GitHub → Settings → Developer settings → Fine-grained tokens → Generate.
        2. Repository access: only your data repo. Permissions: Contents → Read and write. Nothing else.
        3. Paste owner / repo / branch / path + token above → Save & connect → Push now to create the file.
        4. On your other device, open the same hosted page, paste the same details + token, Pull now.

        Security: worst case a leaked token can only edit this one repo's contents. Rotate it anytime; use Forget token on shared machines.
